# 12 — India compliance calendar

> **Status:** authoritative deadline reference. Update whenever a regulation changes.
> **Audience:** finance + tax-ops + compliance dashboard.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-05 (every recurring due date re-verified for FY 2026-27 against current sources; prior review 2026-05-02)

This is the single source of truth for every recurring filing / disclosure / deadline that touches the platform. Any cron-driven artefact also points back here for its cadence.

**FY 2026-27 verification note (2026-06-05):** all monthly/quarterly/annual due dates below were re-confirmed against current publishers for FY 2026-27. **TDS/GST return *due dates* were not changed by the Income-tax Act, 2025** (in force 1-Apr-2026) — but **TDS *form names* changed** for periods on/after 1-Apr-2026 (26Q→Form 140, 27Q→Form 144, 16A→Form 131, 24Q→138, 16→130, 26AS→168, 15CA→145, 15CB→146). GST rates were rationalized to **two slabs (5% + 18%)** w.e.f. 22-Sep-2025 (GST 2.0) — professional/consulting services stay at 18%, so platform GST math is unchanged.

## Recurring quarterly

**Form names change for periods on/after 1-Apr-2026** (Income-tax Rules, 2026): 26Q→**Form 140**, 27Q→**Form 144**, 16A→**Form 131**. The **due dates are unchanged** (verified 2026-06-05). Q4 FY 2025-26 (to 31-Mar-2026) is filed under the *old* names.

| Quarter | Period | Resident return (26Q→Form 140) due | Non-resident return (27Q→Form 144) due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Apr–Jun | 31 Jul | 31 Jul |
| Q2 | Jul–Sep | 31 Oct | 31 Oct |
| Q3 | Oct–Dec | 31 Jan | 31 Jan |
| Q4 | Jan–Mar | **31 May** | **31 May** |

**Form 16A → Form 131** to consultants: within **15 days of the return due date** (i.e. ~15 Aug, 15 Nov, 15 Feb, 15 Jun).

See [doc 04](./04-tds-quarterly-filings.md).

## Advance tax (FY 2026-27)

Verified 2026-06-05. Applies to the *company* (and to consultants in their own filings). Cumulative liability targets:

| Installment due | Cumulative advance tax payable |
|---|---|
| **15 Jun** | 15% |
| **15 Sep** | 45% |
| **15 Dec** | 75% |
| **15 Mar** | 100% |

Interest on shortfall under §424/§425 of the 2025 Act (was §234B/§234C) at 1%/month. New safe-harbour: no interest on the first two installments if ≥12% paid by 15 Jun and ≥36% cumulatively by 15 Sep.

## MSME Form MSME-1 (half-yearly ROC return)

Verified 2026-06-05. Companies Act (MCA), **not** Income-tax — discloses payments to Micro & Small suppliers **outstanding beyond 45 days**. Applies to Practitionist (the company) whenever it owes an MSE supplier past 45 days.

| Half-year period | Filing due |
|---|---|
| Apr–Sep | **31 Oct** |
| Oct–Mar | **30 Apr** |

Penalty: ₹20,000 + ₹1,000/day continuing (cap ₹3 lakh) under Companies Act §405(4). See [doc 03](./03-msme-43b-h.md).

## Penalty / breach risk reference

Section numbers are the 1961-Act citations (use these up to 31-Mar-2026); the 2025 Act renumbers them but the ₹-amounts are unchanged — verify the exact 2025-Act section before quoting in a notice.

| Breach | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Late TDS filing (Form 140 / 144, ex-26Q/27Q) | ₹200/day under Sec 234E (→ §427, 2025 Act), capped at TDS amount |
| Non-filing > 1 year | ₹10,000–₹1,00,000 under Sec 271H |
| Wrong PAN quoted | Separate Sec 271H penalty per row |
| GST TCS not collected (Sec 52) | Equal to TCS not collected (Sec 122(1)(viii)) + 18% p.a. interest (Sec 50(3)) |
| GSTR-8 non-filing | Late fee + interest |
| MSME 43B(h) breach | Expense disallowance in current FY tax computation until paid (MICRO/SMALL only) |
| MSME-1 (ROC) non-filing | ₹20,000 + ₹1,000/day continuing (cap ₹3 lakh), Companies Act §405(4) |
| Advance-tax shortfall | 1%/month interest, §424/§425 (2025 Act; ex-234B/234C) |
| DPDP breach not reported within 72h | Penalty up to ₹250 cr (Schedule, DPDP Act) |
| Consumer Protection grievance SLA breach | Damages + civil court risk via CCPA escalation |
| Cross-border 15CA/CB skipped | Sec 271-I penalty up to ₹1 lakh per non-filing |
