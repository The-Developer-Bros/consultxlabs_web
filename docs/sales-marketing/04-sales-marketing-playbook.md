## 1. Executive summary

This document is the **operational manual** for Familiarise's sales and marketing function in Phase 0 (pre-launch → first 5 paying customers) and Phase 1 (first 10-20 paying customers). It covers:

- Who to hire (intern-first), how much to pay them, how long to keep them on a trial.
- Exact scripts for cold outreach, onboarding calls, objection handling, churn intervention.
- Weekly cadence, KPIs, and promotion criteria.
- The asymmetric-competition playbook for winning against Topmate, Preplaced, GrowthSchool, Metvy.

**The governing constraint:** we must stay profitable in the medium-to-long term while competing against well-funded incumbents. Every marketing rupee must have a traceable return within 90 days. Every sales conversation must either close or teach — no motion without outcome.

**Three-sentence summary:**

1. Phase 0 runs on 2 interns (1 sales/CS + 1 marketing/content) at ₹10-12K/month stipends — total ₹25K/month — focused on converting hand-picked creators + enterprise design partners.
2. Phase 1 converts the best intern to FT CS Lead (₹35-50K) + hires a content marketer (₹30-45K) — channels shift from founder-led outbound to organic content + community.
3. Primary acquisition motion is **founder-led + creator-word-of-mouth in the tech-mentor niche**; paid channels only turn on in Phase 2 after organic proof exists.

You are pre-MVP, 2-3 person team, competing against:

| **upGrad** | $400M+ | 1000+ | TV ads, Google Ads, partnership programs | Enterprise learning, not creator-led |

You cannot out-spend any of them on paid acquisition. You cannot out-hire any of them. You must win by being **categorically different in the eyes of your first 100 customers.**

### 2.2 The positioning statement

> **Familiarise is the India-native creator platform where you keep more of your money and your customers get a better experience.**
>
> **For creators:** 10% commission, UPI settlement (not Stripe FX), integrated video/chat (no Zoom-link gymnastics), 4 service types in one dashboard.
>
> **For teams:** Sponsor your people's learning with Indian compliance built-in, GST invoicing, SSO when you need it, and per-seat or per-credit billing — not another imported LMS.

### 2.3 The three tactical wedges

Every sales or marketing motion must reinforce at least one of these:

1. **"You keep more money."** — the math of UPI vs Stripe FX. Concrete: ₹2,750-3,750/month more in the creator's pocket at ₹50K GMV.
2. **"It just works."** — integrated video + chat + four service types + document review in one place. No tab-switching.
3. **"India-native."** — Razorpay UPI, GST invoices, ITC claims, Net-60 POs. Not a US product with an Indian skin.

## 4. Customer Success / Sales intern — deep dive

### 4.1 Role definition

**Hybrid role during Phase 0.** The intern does both outbound sales conversations (converting hand-picked creators) AND customer success (onboarding + L1 support). This is intentional — at low volume, the same person should close AND onboard because the onboarding call is where renewals are won.

**Reports to:** CEO directly.
**Workspace:** Slack + shared Gmail inbox + Intercom + a Notion page for call notes.
**Stipend:** ₹10-12K/month paid, 3-month contract, with clear conversion criteria to FT CS Lead at ₹35-50K.

### 4.2 Daily responsibilities

| Time block | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 9-10 AM | Review overnight signups + intercom tickets | Triage, assign, respond to L1 |
| 10-12 PM | Outbound sales: LinkedIn / Twitter / WhatsApp to target creators | 10 outreach messages/day, 2-3 replies logged in Notion |
| 12-1 PM | Onboarding calls (30 min each, up to 2/day) with new paying creators | Call notes logged, success metric tagged |
| 2-4 PM | L1 support + canned-response library maintenance | FAQ updates, ticket tag-cluster |
| 4-5 PM | Daily standup with CEO (15 min) + weekly report prep (30 min) | Daily log: calls made, tickets handled, obstacles |

**Weekly deliverable (every Friday):**

- Number of outbound messages sent (goal: 50-70/week).
- Number of qualified replies (goal: 10-15/week).
- Number of onboarding calls completed (goal: 5-8/week at Phase 0, 10-15/week at Phase 1).
- NPS pulse (2 questions via email after every 2nd session per creator).
- Top 5 ticket themes + recommended product/ops changes.

### 4.3 Success metrics (90-day trial)

| Metric | Target | Failure threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified creator conversations | 30+ total | < 15 |
| Paying creators acquired (direct attribution) | 10+ | < 5 |
| Onboarding call coverage | 100% of new paying creators | < 80% |
| NPS average | > 40 | < 25 |
| First-response time p50 | < 4 business hours | > 8 business hours |
| Ticket tag-cluster actionable | 80%+ tickets tagged with clear category | < 60% |

**Conversion decision (day 90):** if all targets met, offer FT CS Lead role at ₹35-50K. If 3+ metrics failing, end contract, hire replacement intern.

### 4.4 Outbound sales scripts

These are **templates**, not verbatim. The intern must personalize every message with 1-2 specific data points about the creator (their Topmate profile, their Twitter bio, a recent tweet, etc.).

#### Script 1: Cold LinkedIn — active Topmate creator

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw your Topmate profile with [X consultations / Y subscription members] — looks like you're building something real in [their niche, e.g. "product management mentoring"].
>
> I'm building Familiarise, a creator platform designed for Indian creators like you. Few quick things that might matter:
>
> 1. **You keep more.** We charge 10% commission with UPI settlement — no Stripe, no FX conversion. At your volume, that's likely ₹2-4K/month more in your pocket than Topmate.
> 2. **It just works.** Integrated video + chat + document review + webinars — all in one dashboard. No Zoom link copy-paste, no chasing payment statuses.
>
> We're letting creators migrating from Topmate onto a **5% commission plan for 3 months** (first 50 creators). No contracts, no lock-ins.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? I'd love to show you the dashboard and see if it's a fit.
>
> — [Name], Familiarise

**Do NOT:**

- Disparage Topmate or other competitors by name. Comparisons yes, attacks no.
- Make false claims (e.g. "zero fees" when cards are 2.36%).
- Use generic "Hope you're doing well" openers. Cut to the specific signal.

#### Script 2: Cold Twitter DM — high-engagement niche creator

> Hey [Name] — your thread on [specific recent tweet topic] got me thinking.
>
> I run Familiarise (Indian creator platform, 10% commission, UPI-native, integrated video). Your audience feels like exactly who'd pay for 1:1 sessions with you.
>
> Would love to show you what a creator dashboard looks like when it's built for how Indian creators actually work. 15 min?

#### Script 3: Warm intro (from founder network)

> Hi [Name], [Mutual connection] mentioned you've been thinking about monetizing your [podcast / newsletter / expertise] more seriously.
>
> I'd love to show you Familiarise — it's what we'd built if we were frustrated with every existing creator tool (we were). Integrated video, UPI payouts, four service types in one place. 10% commission with a migration discount if you're already on another platform.
>
> Free to hop on a 20-minute call this week?

### 4.5 Onboarding call script (30 min)

Run every new paying creator through this. Record with consent; use for product feedback + testimonial raw material.

**Minutes 0-5: Rapport + goal**

> "Thanks for hopping on. Before we get into the platform, I'd love to hear: what made you decide to try Familiarise? What are you hoping to get out of it in the next 30 days?"

Listen. Log the answer. This is your positioning gold — use their exact words in marketing.

**Minutes 5-15: Platform walkthrough**

Focus on **their** use case, not a generic demo:

- If they said "1:1 consultations" — walk through creating a plan, setting availability, booking flow.
- If they said "webinars" — walk through event creation, slot allocation, attendee management.
- If they said "subscriptions" — walk through recurring billing + delivery cadence.

Show the **creator dashboard** (revenue, upcoming sessions, past bookings) in their live account. Don't show enterprise features or anything irrelevant.

**Minutes 15-25: First booking commitment**

> "Let's make sure your first booking happens this week. Who's the one person you'd feel most confident asking to test this with you? Let's send them a link right now."

Actually send the link during the call. This is the single most predictive moment of retention — a creator who shares their link on the onboarding call converts at > 70%; one who doesn't drops to < 30%.

**Minutes 25-30: Expectations + next touch**

> "I'll check in with you after your first booking to hear how it went. If anything goes sideways — video issue, payment delay, anything — WhatsApp me directly at [number]. My goal is that you feel this platform is actually fighting for you."

Log the call in Notion:

- Creator's goal (in their words).
- Niche.
- First-booking commitment (who, when).
- Red flags (hesitation, objections, comparisons to other platforms).

### 4.6 Objection handling

The top 5 objections + scripts:

#### Objection: "I'm already on Topmate, why switch?"

> Totally fair. Two reasons most creators have switched:
>
> 1. **Money math.** Topmate routes through Stripe + currency conversion — you lose ~6-8% to FX on top of the 10% commission. On Familiarise via UPI, you lose 0% to gateway. At your volume that's ₹X,000 more/month in your pocket.
> 2. **The link-in-bio isn't a platform.** We do 4 service types — 1:1, webinars, classes, subscriptions — in one dashboard with integrated video. Topmate is mostly consultations + Zoom links.
>
> You don't have to leave Topmate. Many creators run both during a 2-month test. If we earn your primary listing after that, great. If not, no loss.

#### Objection: "I don't have an audience yet."

> Fair. Familiarise isn't a discovery platform — we won't magically bring you traffic. But what we will do is make sure that when your audience DOES find you, you can monetize immediately with integrated video + chat + document review, not a Calendly + Zoom + Razorpay duct-tape stack.
>
> The 10% commission means you keep more of what you do earn. The migration bonus (5% for 3 months if you're on Topmate) helps you retain more during the growth phase.
>
> If building audience is your main blocker, I'll actually introduce you to [name of our CS intern / content lead] who can walk you through content strategies that creators on our platform use.

#### Objection: "Your platform is too new / too small."

> You're right — we're early. Here's the honest trade-off:
>
> - **Early creator benefit:** you shape the product. We literally built [feature X] because a creator asked. That won't happen on Topmate — they have 300K creators; your voice is one of many.
> - **Risk:** yes, we could fold. We have [runway length] in the bank. Our burn is low. We're not a rocket ship that crashes.
> - **Mitigation:** you don't leave your existing platform. You run parallel for 60 days. No cost to try.
>
> The founding 50 creators get 0% commission for the first 3 months — we're putting our money where our mouth is.

#### Objection: "Your video quality / reliability is unproven."

> Valid concern. We use Stream.io under the hood — the same infrastructure that powers [notable Stream customers, e.g. major apps]. In 3 months of beta we've had zero session-breaking outages.
>
> That said: you shouldn't take my word. Your first session is the test. We guarantee that if the video breaks due to our platform (not your wifi), you get a full refund for that session AND a make-good credit. Write it in an email — we'll honor it.

#### Objection: "Commission is the same as Topmate, what's different?"

> Headline is the same — 10%. Effective is very different.
>
> Topmate: 10% commission + ~2.9% Stripe + ~2-3% FX markup = **effectively 15-17% gone.**
>
> Familiarise: 10% commission + ~0% UPI + ₹0 FX = **effectively 10%.**
>
> Plus the Topmate-migration discount: 5% for 3 months if you show us your active Topmate profile. That's 10%+ saved for 3 months, then standard tier after.

### 4.7 Customer success day-to-day

Beyond outbound + onboarding, the CS intern owns:

**Ticket handling (L1):**

- Password reset, can't-find-the-button, how-do-I-X → FAQ + canned response.
- Bug that dev needs to fix → reproduce, write clean repro, assign to dev via Linear (L2).
- Escalation to CEO: anything involving refunds > ₹5,000, creator suspension, press/PR, legal threats.

**Proactive outreach:**

- 48 hours after first paid booking: "How did it go? Any issues?" (via WhatsApp — higher reply rate than email).
- 14 days after signup with no paid booking: "Is there something blocking you from sharing your Familiarise link? Happy to jump on a quick call."
- 60 days after signup: NPS survey + quarterly feedback form.

**Churn intervention (any creator with 30+ days of no activity):**

> Hey [Name], I noticed you haven't booked a session in the last 30 days. Before I write this off as "life got busy," I wanted to ask — is there anything about the platform that didn't work the way you expected?
>
> If the answer is "no, just busy" — totally understand, no action needed. If it's "actually I hit [specific problem]" — tell me. We fix our actual blockers fast.

Log response + outcome in Notion. Every churn interaction produces either a fix, a testimonial, or a clean farewell.

### 6.3 Profitability math

Phase 0 monthly team cost (interns + existing team):

```
CEO (equity, no cash): ₹0
Shubham (dev): ₹15K (current; revisit per HIRING_PLAN.md)
```

**This is the Phase 0 goal line.** The interns' incentive structure is aligned to get us there.

## 10. Scaling signals

### 10.1 When to hire beyond Phase 1

| Signal | Hire to consider |
|---|---|
| CS Lead handles > 50 tickets/week solo | Add CS intern #2 |
| Content marketer can't keep up with 5 posts/week | Add content writer (contract or FT) |
| Paid ads becoming measurably profitable (CAC < 0.5 × LTV) | Growth marketer (FT) |
| Enterprise pipeline > 10 qualified opps | Enterprise AE |
| Support L2 escalations > 5/week | Junior dev doing 20% support rotation |

### 10.2 When to pull back

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Burn > ₹3L/month with < ₹2L MRR | Freeze all hiring, reduce intern count |
| Churn > 10%/month for 2+ consecutive months | Pause marketing spend, focus on retention |
| CAC > 50% of LTV | Pause outbound, pivot to organic-only |
| Stream.io cliff triggers before ₹10L GMV | Reduce team to < 5 FT, stretch Maker tier |

## 11. What NOT to do

Direct list of things to avoid, informed by watching other pre-MVP Indian startups fail:

1. **Don't run paid ads before organic works.** Paid is a multiplier. If your organic funnel doesn't convert, paid just burns money faster.
2. **Don't hire a "growth lead" or "head of marketing" in Phase 0 or 1.** Generic titles. Hire for a specific output (content, community, outbound).
3. **Don't make the first enterprise customer a giant company.** Too much customization demand. Target 30-100 employee companies as design partners.
4. **Don't discount prices to win deals.** Discounting signals "we're desperate." Use the Topmate-migration discount as a structured offer only; never as an ad-hoc negotiation tactic.
5. **Don't let Shelu's commission-only compensation persist past 10 closed deals.** Commission-only sales lead to misaligned incentives (they oversell, customer later feels duped). Move Shelu to ₹5-10K base + commission once patterns show.
6. **Don't let interns make product decisions.** They tag + cluster tickets; CEO decides the roadmap. Never the other way around.
7. **Don't ghost creators who don't convert immediately.** A non-converter today might be a convert in 3 months. Keep a "not now" nurture list with quarterly touchpoints.
8. **Don't dismiss negative feedback.** Every 1-star mention, every churn exit-interview, every comparison-to-competitors note must be logged + weekly-reviewed by CEO.
9. **Don't over-engineer support tooling.** Zendesk/Salesforce/HubSpot are overkill in Phase 0. Gmail + Intercom free/starter tier is enough.
10. **Don't compete on features with well-funded incumbents.** Compete on positioning, trust, and India-native product decisions.
