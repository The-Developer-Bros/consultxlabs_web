## If You Read Nothing Else: 5 Actions for the Next 30 Days

1. **Send 200 personalized LinkedIn outreach messages** to senior tech professionals. 10/day, 5 days/week, 4 weeks. This is the single highest-leverage action.
2. **Complete 10 demo calls** and onboard your first 5 founding consultants with profiles, services, availability, and pricing configured.
3. **Be the first consultee yourself** — book and pay for sessions with your first 3 consultants. Leave real reviews. Seed the marketplace.
4. **Record a 5-minute Loom demo** of the full booking flow. Embed on landing page. Post on LinkedIn.
5. **Set up your free marketing stack** — Google Sheets CRM, Buffer for social scheduling, Tally.so for consultant application form. Total cost: ₹0.

Everything else in this document is context for these 5 actions.

## Part 2: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Supply Side — Consultant ICP

**Primary ICP: The "Ready Creator"**
- 5-15 years professional experience
- Already creating content (LinkedIn posts, blogs, YouTube, Twitter)
- 1K+ LinkedIn connections or social followers
- Has been asked for advice/mentorship informally
- Frustrated with current monetization options (TopMate's fees, Zoom link chaos, WhatsApp advisory for free)
- Domains: tech, finance, product management, design, career coaching

**Secondary ICP: The "Latent Expert"**
- 10-20 years experience, deep domain knowledge
- NOT creating content yet — but would if given a platform
- Examples: senior CAs with tax advisory expertise, corporate lawyers advising startups informally, specialist doctors giving second opinions via WhatsApp
- Higher onboarding friction but higher lifetime value

**Disqualified Profiles (Do NOT Recruit)**
- Astrologers, tarot readers (brand dilution, attracts wrong consultee base)
- Anyone who cannot verify expertise (no LinkedIn, no portfolio, no credentials)
- Professionals with <2 years experience (not enough expertise to charge for)
- Anyone who only wants to sell courses/PDFs (use Gumroad/Teachable instead)

**ICP Scoring Rubric**

Score each potential consultant 1-10 on these dimensions:

| Dimension | 1-3 (Low) | 4-6 (Medium) | 7-10 (High) |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Audience size | <500 connections | 500-2K | 2K+ LinkedIn, 1K+ social |
| Engagement quality | Rarely posts | Posts monthly | Posts weekly, gets comments |
| Domain demand | Niche/low-search | Moderate demand | High search volume, trending |
| Platform fit | Uses our 1 feature | Uses 2 features | Will use 3-4 service types |
| Activity likelihood | Unclear commitment | "Maybe" | "Yes, I want to start" |

**Minimum score to actively pursue: 25/50**
**Founding member threshold: 35/50**

### Demand Side — Consultee ICP

**Primary Personas:**

| Persona | Description | Budget | Where to Find |
|---------|-------------|--------|---------------|
| **The Interview Prepper** | Engineering students/junior devs preparing for FAANG/product companies | ₹500-3,000/session | College WhatsApp groups, r/developersIndia, LinkedIn |
| **The Career Transitioner** | 3-7 year professionals switching domains/roles | ₹1,000-5,000/session | LinkedIn, Twitter, career forums |
| **The Startup Founder** | Early-stage founders needing tech/legal/finance advisory | ₹2,000-10,000/session | Startup WhatsApp groups, Y Combinator India, Twitter |
| **The NRI** | Indians abroad needing India-specific advice (tax, legal, career) | ₹2,000-15,000/session | Diaspora LinkedIn groups, NRI forums |
| **The SMB Owner** | Small business owners needing professional advice (GST, legal compliance) | ₹1,000-5,000/session | Local business networks, Google search |

**Secondary Personas:**
- College students in placement season (Sep-Mar) — high volume, low price
- Working professionals seeking upskilling — medium volume, medium price
- Employees seeking career coaching — medium volume, medium price

## Part 4: Vertical Expansion Timeline

### Updated Sequence (ICAI Liberalization Factored In)

| Phase | Vertical | Timeline | Key Trigger |
|-------|----------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Tech/Engineering** | Month 1-3 | Founder's network, highest digital adoption |
| 2 | **Finance/CA** | Month 3-5 | **ICAI advertising liberalization April 1, 2026** |
| 3 | **Creative Arts** | Month 5-7 | Instagram/YouTube presence makes acquisition easy |
| 4 | **Legal** | Month 7-9 | Preparation needed for BCI Rule 36 compliance |
| 5 | **Medicine/Health** | Month 9-12 | Start with wellness/nutrition (lighter regulation) |

### Why This Order

**Tech first (Month 1-3):** You're a technical founder. Your network is tech. Your LinkedIn connections are engineers. The product was built for this vertical. Path of least resistance.

**Finance/CA second (Month 3-5):** The ICAI advertising liberalization on April 1, 2026 is a once-in-a-generation timing opportunity. For decades, CAs were restricted from digital marketing. Starting April 2026, they can. CAs will be looking for digital platforms with zero established playbook. If you're there first with a polished offering, you capture the early adopters. Tax filing season (July-September) creates a natural demand spike. Onboard CAs in April-June (off-season), they'll be ready for the July rush.

**Creative Arts third (Month 5-7):** Lowest regulatory friction of any non-tech vertical. Creatives are already on Instagram/YouTube — digital-native audience. Portfolio review via document sharing is a killer feature no competitor offers. Easy acquisition because the target audience is already online and discoverable.

**Legal fourth (Month 7-9):** High willingness to pay (₹1,000-5,000 per session) but BCI Rule 36 advertising restrictions require careful positioning. Need time to build compliant messaging and disclaimer frameworks. Document review for contracts is a natural fit but needs legal review before marketing.

**Medicine/Health last (Month 9-12):** Highest regulatory barriers (Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020, informed consent, registration requirements). Start with non-doctor experts (nutritionists, wellness coaches, psychologists) to reduce regulatory complexity. Doctors added after compliance framework is proven.

## Part 5: Competitive Positioning

### The One-Liner

> **"10% commission. UPI payments. Built-in video. You keep more, you earn more."**

### Positioning by Audience

**To Tech Consultants:**
> "Stop stitching together TopMate + Zoom + Stripe + spreadsheets. One platform: 1:1s, subscriptions, webinars, classes. Integrated HD video. Automatic recording. Document review. 10% commission with zero forex loss."

**To CAs/Finance Professionals:**
> "ICAI now allows digital marketing. Familiarise is the platform built for it. Offer tax advisory sessions, monthly retainer subscriptions, GST masterclass webinars — all in one place. UPI payments, transparent payouts, ₹0 platform fees for founding members."

**To Career Seekers/Consultees:**
> "Find India's top experts. Browse by specialty. Read verified reviews. Book a session or free trial. Everything happens in one app — no Zoom links, no email chains."

### Why We Win vs. Specific Competitors

**vs. TopMate:** Their effective take rate is 16-18% (10% + Stripe 3% + forex 3-5%). Ours is 10% flat via UPI. At ₹1L/month GMV, consultants save ₹5,500-7,500/month. TopMate's trust score is 51.2/100 (Scamadviser). We have integrated video (they use Zoom links). We have 4 service types (they have 1-2). See `docs/competition/03-feature-battlecard.md` for full comparison.

**vs. SuperProfile:** ₹99/month flat fee is unsustainable — will inevitably raise prices. Shallow features (no video, no session management, no collaboration). When SuperProfile raises prices, be ready to capture displaced creators.

**vs. GrowthSchool:** Severe reputation damage ("scam" actively circulating). Instructor lock-in (no price control, no brand ownership). We're the platform for instructors who want to own their business.

**vs. Preplaced:** 20% commission (double ours). 600 mentors only. Tech niche only. No integrated video. Direct poaching target.

For detailed competitive analysis, see `docs/competition/01-threat-matrix.md`.

## Part 7: Revenue Model & Break-Even

For detailed pricing analysis, see `docs/competition/04-pricing-strategy.md`.

### Summary

| Phase | Commission | Gateway Fee (UPI) | Effective Take Rate |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Founding (0-3 months) | 0% | 0% | 0% |
| Launch (1-3 months) | 10% | 0% | 10% |
| Migration (TopMate switchers) | 5% for 3 months | 0% | 5% |
| Growth (4-9 months) | 10% standard, 8% for >₹50K/mo | 0% | 8-10% |
| Maturity (10+ months) | Tiered: 12-20% | 0% | 12-20% |

### Break-Even Math

**Current monthly costs:** ~₹20K (see `docs/stream/00-pricing-overview.md` for Stream costs; overall SaaS burn ~₹10-12K pre-GST)

At 10% blended commission rate:
- Break-even GMV: ₹20K / 0.10 = **₹2L/month**
- Transactions needed: ~200 at ₹1,000 avg
- Active consultants needed: ~20 (at 10 sessions/month each)
- **Achievable by Month 2-3**

### Stream.io Cost Cliff Warning

At ₹8.5L revenue (or $100K funding, or >5 team members):
- Stream.io jumps from ₹0 → ₹36K/month
- New break-even: ~₹5.6L GMV/month
- **Plan:** Grow organically past cliff. At ₹8.5L revenue, GMV should be ₹85L+ — easily covers the cost.
