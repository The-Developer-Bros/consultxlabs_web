## Category 2: CA / GST Registration Consultant

**Priority:** EXISTENTIAL | **Budget:** ₹1,500–3,000 one-time + ₹2,000–5,000/month | **Hire By:** Week 1

**Why Critical:** Section 24(x) CGST Act mandates GST registration for e-commerce operators regardless of turnover. Familiarise almost certainly qualifies. No GSTIN = cannot legally collect payments.

### Critical Tax Intelligence (Research Findings)

| Issue | Finding | Action Required |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **GST Registration** | **Mandatory** under Section 24(x) — e-commerce operators have no turnover threshold | Register before first transaction |
| **TDS Provision** | **Section 194-O** (0.1% on payouts to consultants) — NOT 194-J | Verify with CA; update TDS logic in `tds-service.ts` |
| **Section 44AD** | **Likely NOT applicable** — commission/brokerage income is explicitly excluded from 44AD. CFO plan assumption may be wrong | Get written CA opinion before entity filing |
| **TCS on payouts** | 0.5% GST TCS if classified as ECO (Section 52 CGST); GSTR-8 monthly | Implement post-GSTIN |
| **LUT for exports** | File Letter of Undertaking for zero-rating international transactions | File before first international payment |
